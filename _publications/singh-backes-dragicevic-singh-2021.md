## Abstract

This paper investigates shadowing properties for nonautonomous and nonlinear dynamical systems with impulsive effects. The results extend classical shadowing theory to systems with discontinuities.

**Keywords:** Shadowing, nonautonomous dynamics, impulses, nonlinear dynamics
