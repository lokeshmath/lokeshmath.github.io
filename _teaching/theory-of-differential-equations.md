**Offered:** Prior to 2025–26

### Course Description
A theoretical treatment of ordinary differential equations, including existence and uniqueness theorems, linear systems, stability analysis, and an introduction to partial differential equations.

### Typical Syllabus
- First-order ODEs: existence, uniqueness, qualitative behaviour
- Linear systems: matrix methods, phase portraits
- Nonlinear systems: linearisation, stability, Lyapunov methods
- Boundary value problems and Green’s functions
- Series solutions and special functions
- Introduction to PDEs (if covered)
