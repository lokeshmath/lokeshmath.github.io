**Offered:** Prior to 2025–26

### Course Description
A rigorous first course in analysis of real-valued functions of a single variable. Covered the real number system, sequences, series, limits, continuity, differentiation, and Riemann integration, with an emphasis on proof writing.

### Typical Syllabus
- Real numbers: completeness, supremum and infimum
- Sequences and their convergence, Cauchy sequences
- Infinite series, tests for convergence
- Limits of functions, continuity, uniform continuity
- Differentiability, Mean Value Theorem, Taylor’s theorem
- Riemann integration, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
