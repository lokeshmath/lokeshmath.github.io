## 👋 About Me

Hello! I am **Dr. Lokesh Singh**, an **Assistant Professor** in the **Department of Mathematics** at **Amity University Punjab, Mohali**.

My research focuses on **Differential Equations**, with a particular interest in their applications to real-world problems in science and engineering. I am passionate about teaching and mentoring students in mathematical concepts and their practical applications.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

| Area | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **Ordinary Differential Equations** | Existence and uniqueness of solutions |
| **NonAutonomous Differential Equations** | Solutions in Stable and Unstable directions |
| **Shadowing Property** | Existence of Solutions |
