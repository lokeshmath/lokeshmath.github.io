## 📖 Course Description

A rigorous treatment of set theory, logic, and metric spaces, forming the backbone of real analysis and topology.

---

## 📋 Syllabus Outline

- Sets, relations, functions, cardinality
- Axiom of choice and equivalents (overview)
- Metric spaces: definition, open/closed sets, convergence, continuity
- Completeness, compactness, connectedness
- Banach fixed‑point theorem and applications
- Introduction to topological spaces (time permitting)
