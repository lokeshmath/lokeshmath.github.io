**Offered:** Prior to 2025–26

### Course Description
An advanced undergraduate / beginning graduate course on Lebesgue measure and integration, abstract measure spaces, and the foundations of probability theory.

### Typical Syllabus
- Lebesgue measure on \( \mathbb{R} \)
- Measurable functions and integration
- Convergence theorems (monotone, dominated)
- \( L^p \) spaces
- Product measures and Fubini’s theorem
- Abstract measure spaces and the Radon‑Nikodym theorem
- Connection to probability (if covered)
