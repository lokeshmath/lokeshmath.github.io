**Offered:** Prior to 2025–26

### Course Description
An introduction to vector spaces, linear transformations, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, and inner product spaces. Emphasised both theoretical foundations and computational aspects relevant to engineering and the sciences.

### Typical Syllabus
- Systems of linear equations and Gaussian elimination
- Matrix algebra, inverses, determinants
- Vector spaces, subspaces, basis, dimension
- Linear transformations and their matrix representations
- Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, diagonalisation
- Orthogonality and least squares
- Applications (if included): linear programming, differential equations, computer graphics
