**Offered:** 2025–26 Even Semester  
**Audience:** B.Sc. Life Sciences, B.Tech. Biotechnology

### Course Description
An applied statistics course focusing on experimental design, hypothesis testing, and biological data analysis, with hands‑on computational work.

### Syllabus Outline
- Descriptive statistics and data visualisation
- Probability distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Normal)
- Sampling distributions, Central Limit Theorem
- Estimation and confidence intervals
- Hypothesis testing (t‑tests, chi‑squared, ANOVA)
- Correlation and regression
- Introduction to non‑parametric methods

### Teaching Materials
- R/Python lab sessions
- Case study assignments (clinical trials, ecology)
- Group mini‑project on analysing a biological dataset
- Step‑by‑step software guides

*Lab scripts and datasets are on the course LMS.*
