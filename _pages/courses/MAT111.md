**Offered:** 2025–26 Even Semester  
**Audience:** First‑year B.Tech. students (continuation of Mathematics I)

### Course Description
Extends calculus to several variables, introduces linear algebra, and covers ordinary differential equations – essential for all engineering streams.

### Syllabus Outline
- Multivariable Calculus: partial derivatives, maxima/minima, multiple integrals
- Linear Algebra: matrices, determinants, eigenvalues/eigenvectors
- Vector Calculus: gradient, divergence, curl, line/surface integrals
- Ordinary Differential Equations: first‑order, linear with constant coefficients, applications

### Teaching Materials
- Lecture notes (typed)
- Tutorials with engineering application problems
- MATLAB/Python scripts for visualisation
- Previous year question bank
