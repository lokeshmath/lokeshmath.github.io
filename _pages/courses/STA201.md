## 📖 Course Description

A comprehensive introduction to the theory of probability and statistics, covering descriptive statistics, probability theory, discrete and continuous random variables, bivariate distributions, and their applications in engineering and scientific problems.

---

## 📋 Syllabus Outline

### Unit I: Descriptive Statistics and Probability
- Measures of Central Tendency
- Measures of Dispersion
- Skewness and Kurtosis
- Definitions of Probability (Classical, Statistical & Axiomatic)
- Conditional Probability and Independence
- Bayes' Theorem and Applications

### Unit II: Random Variable – Discrete Distributions
- Random Variables: Types, PMF & PDF
- Expectation and Variance of Random Variables
- Uniform and Bernoulli Distributions
- Binomial Distribution
- Poisson Distribution
- Geometric Distribution
- Negative Binomial and Hypergeometric Distributions

### Unit III: Random Variable – Continuous Distributions
- Uniform Distribution
- Exponential Distribution
- Gamma Distribution
- Beta Distribution
- Normal Distribution
- Transformation of Random Variable (Single Variable)

### Unit IV: Bivariate Random Variables
- Introduction to Bivariate Random Variables
- Joint Probability Distribution
- Marginal Probability Distribution
- Covariance
- Correlation
