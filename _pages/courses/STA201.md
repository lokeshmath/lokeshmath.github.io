**Offered:** 2025–26 Odd Semester, 2026–27 Odd Semester  
**Audience:** B.Tech. students (multiple branches)

### Course Description
A comprehensive introduction to probability theory and statistical inference, equipping engineers with the tools to model uncertainty, analyse data, and make data‑driven decisions.

### Detailed Syllabus
**Unit I: Descriptive Statistics & Probability**  
Measures of central tendency, dispersion, skewness, kurtosis. Definitions of probability (classical, statistical, axiomatic). Conditional probability, independence, Bayes’ theorem and applications.

**Unit II: Discrete Random Variables**  
Random variables (discrete/continuous), pmf, pdf. Expectation, moment generating functions. Distributions: Uniform, Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, Geometric, Negative Binomial, Hypergeometric.

**Unit III: Continuous Distributions**  
Uniform, Exponential, Gamma, Beta, Weibull, Normal, Lognormal. Transformation of random variables, probability integral transform.

**Unit IV: Joint Distributions & Regression**  
Multiple random variables, joint/marginal distributions. Bivariate transformations, covariance, correlation, simple linear regression.
