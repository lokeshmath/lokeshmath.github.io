**Offered:** 2025–26 Odd Semester, 2026–27 Odd Semester  
**Audience:** B.Sc. / B.Tech. Biotechnology and allied programmes

### Course Description
Mathematics tailored to biological contexts: modelling population growth, enzyme kinetics, genetic frequencies, and basic statistical analysis of biological data.

### Syllabus Outline
- Functions and Graphs in Biology
- Limits, Growth and Decay Models
- Differentiation: rates in biology, optimisation
- Integration: areas, cumulative processes
- Introduction to Differential Equations (logistic growth, predator‑prey)
- Basic Probability and Statistics for life sciences

### Teaching Materials
- Context‑rich lecture slides with biological case studies
- Lab‑style data analysis tasks using Python/R
- Tutorials with real‑world datasets
- Mini‑project on modelling a biological phenomenon

*All materials are available on the LMS; sample code notebooks are provided.*
