**Offered:** 2026–27 Odd Semester  
**Audience:** Advanced undergraduate mathematics / B.Tech. (Engineering Physics, CS theory stream)

### Course Description
A rigorous treatment of set theory, logic, and metric spaces, forming the backbone of real analysis and topology.

### Syllabus Outline
- Sets, relations, functions, cardinality
- Axiom of choice and equivalents (overview)
- Metric spaces: definition, open/closed sets, convergence, continuity
- Completeness, compactness, connectedness
- Banach fixed‑point theorem and applications
- Introduction to topological spaces (time permitting)

### Teaching Materials
- Detailed lecture notes (proof‑based)
- Weekly problem sets (theoretical, some counter‑example hunt)
- Seminar‑style discussion sessions
- Take‑home exams focusing on rigorous proofs

*Lecture notes and additional reading lists are on the LMS. Office hours are encouraged.*
